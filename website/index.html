The companion app

Everything you'd do if you lived down the street.

EasyLink Care runs on your phone. It's how you set up their home screen, manage their medications, and find out — immediately — when something's wrong.

Remote setup

Choose which buttons appear on their home screen, add speed-dial contacts and photos, and build medication schedules — all from your phone. No more walking someone through settings menus over the phone.

Medication adherence

Not just reminders. See that the noon dose was actually taken, spot a pattern of missed evenings, and get a nudge before it becomes a hospital visit.

Instant alerts

SOS presses, detected falls, missed doses, and a phone about to die — pushed to you the moment they happen, with the whole family notified at once.

How it works

Two apps. One 6-digit code.

Setup takes about five minutes, and only one person needs to be comfortable with a phone.

1

Install the launcher

EasyLink Launcher replaces the home screen on the Android phone they already own. Big buttons, high contrast, no app drawer to get lost in.

2

Pair with a code

Their phone shows a 6-digit code. You type it into EasyLink Care once, and the two phones are linked — securely, and for good.

3

Set it up and step back

Configure everything from your phone, then let it run. You'll only hear from it when something actually needs you.

Pricing
